Meta Product Browsing Not Sending Traffic to Your Website

Meta product browsing is not sending traffic to your website when a tap opens a product screen inside Facebook or Instagram first. Sessions fall. Link clicks often do not.

JBy Jack Goldsmith · Published 19 November 2026 · 6 min read

ContentsWhy Meta product browsing is not sending trafficWhy Meta ads ROAS dropped in late October 2026Are Advantage+ catalogue ads skipping Shopify?How to send the tap back to your shopWhen you should leave product browsing onFrequently Asked Questions

If Meta product browsing is not sending traffic to my website, the ads are often still running. From 28 October 2026, a tap on many existing Facebook and Instagram ads can open a product screen inside the app before the shop. People who leave that screen never load Shopify or GA4, so sessions fall while link clicks in Ads Manager do not. A purchase can still happen if they continue, which is why traffic and orders disagree.

By Jack Goldsmith, Founder & Performance Marketer, Social Surge · 19 November 2026

In the accounts we manage, the first move is to cut the Meta budget. Separate a missing session from a missing order before you do. A quieter GA4 report is not, by itself, a worse week.

Why Meta product browsing is not sending traffic

Meta's help page on Advantage+ creative describes Add product browsing as a personalised screen with product details and offers, shown when someone taps the image or video, before they go on to buy. It says the enhancement may send people to Facebook or Instagram first. Not every tap takes the detour, so the session drop is usually partial.

Until mid-October you turned this on yourself. Meta then told advertisers it would add product browsing to existing ads from 28 October 2026, unless they opted out before that date. Meta has not published the notice. The Keyword recorded the screenshot. After 28 October the opt-out is closed, and the switch is on each ad.

It sits in two places. Under Advantage+ creative it is Add product browsing. Under Destination, personalised destinations lists it beside Shops, optimise website destination and browser add-ons, on Sales campaigns that convert on the website. Meta's help says some enhancements may be on by default. The help pages do not name 28 October. The notice did, and it never said later ads start switched off. Check every duplicate and every Black Friday ad.

Why Meta ads ROAS dropped in late October 2026

Your pixel, the Conversions API, GA4 and Shopify only fire on your domain. Ads Manager can still count the tap, and a purchase if the person buys later. Link clicks stay flat or rise. Landing-page views and GA4 sessions fall. Only purchases and revenue are ROAS.

A wider gap between link clicks and landing-page views fits an in-app stop, or a dead pixel. Check Shopify first. That is event match quality, not this setting.

Meta's notice said about 3% more conversions on average with in-app browsing. The footnote, as reported with the notice, put that on a test from 6 to 21 August 2026 across about 117,700 advertisers in ecommerce, consumer packaged goods and retail. That is Meta's figure. It does not say what a conversion was worth, and the average hides stores that did worse.

An illustration, not a client: if 300 of 1,000 taps stop in the app, sessions fall about 30%, and ROAS falls only if those people would have bought. Compare orders for 1-27 October with 28 October onward. Flat orders: leave the budget. Fewer orders at the same spend: turn the setting off, and wait before you touch the cost cap. Moving spend to Google because sessions fell, while orders held, is the wrong fix: see Google Ads vs Meta Ads.

Our published account average is 18.3x ROAS, with floors of 13x and above in cycling, 12x and above in fishing and 14x and above in outdoor, and a best month of 35.3x. That does not forecast a campaign which has lost its landing-page views. Judge profit after VAT, postage and returns with the break-even ROAS calculator, and read results on the case studies page.

Are Advantage+ catalogue ads skipping Shopify?

Product browsing is not a catalogue ad, and they can run together. That is why Advantage+ catalogue ads look as if they are skipping Shopify. Catalogue ads send people toward the product link in the feed. Product browsing can insert an in-app screen first, including on a single image or video once a catalogue is connected. More carousel delivery is Meta building that step, not Shopify blocking the click.

On Shopify the screen uses the catalogue the sales channel syncs. A stale price or a sold-out size shows first, and anyone who continues can bounce. Clean the feed if you leave browsing on. Turning it off does not fix a wrong price. If sessions still arrive and purchases have stopped in Ads Manager, read why Meta ads are not converting instead. This setting explains missing sessions while purchases still record.

How to send the tap back to your shop

The notice opt-out only worked before 28 October. After that, change the ad. In Ads Manager, edit it, and turn off Add product browsing under Advantage+ creative. Under Destination, then Personalised destinations, turn product browsing off if it is listed. Shops is a separate switch: turn that off too if you wanted the website. Publish. On a large account, bulk edit. Then check duplicates and new Black Friday ads, because a copy can inherit the default.

Compare landing-page views, GA4 sessions from facebook and instagram, and Shopify orders from Meta, for 14 days either side of the change. One change, then a week of similar spend, before you also cut budget. If UTMs vanish, open a product URL that did arrive. If it still carries them, the missing visits stopped in the app.

When you should leave product browsing on

Leave it on when orders and margin held and only sessions fell. Meta's 3% is an average, not a promise. A clean catalogue and a simple product page can be on the right side of it. Turning it off to fill GA4, then losing orders, costs more than the tidy chart is worth.

Turn it off when the page does the selling: a bundle, a gift guide, a fit note, or a sale page you are testing. A cycling shop sending a rear light or a rack there loses the fit answer. An outdoor shop sending a jacket loses the size guide. Do not leave browsing on and point the ad at a URL outside the catalogue.

With Black Friday a week away, make one change and stop. A second change the same week is unreadable. Meta Ads management is a flat £1,000 a month, and Google Ads is £1,500 if you run both. Fees are on the pricing page. To read the split off the live account, start with the free audit.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is Meta product browsing not sending traffic to my website?

From 28 October 2026 Meta can open a product screen on Facebook or Instagram after the tap, before your shop. People who leave on that screen never become a Shopify or GA4 session, so site traffic falls while Ads Manager link clicks can stay flat. It is an Advantage+ creative setting, Add product browsing, and it may also sit under Personalised destinations. Not every tap takes the detour.

Why did my Meta ads ROAS drop in late October 2026?

ROAS falls only if orders or revenue fell, not because sessions fell. The in-app screen is invisible to your pixel and to GA4. Meta's notice said about 3% more conversions on average in an August 2026 test. That is Meta's figure, it is an average, and it does not say the extra orders were worth as much. Compare Meta-attributed orders before and after 28 October before you cut budget.

Are Advantage+ catalogue ads skipping my Shopify store?

Catalogue ads and product browsing are different, and they can run together. Catalogue ads send people toward the product link in the feed. Product browsing can insert an in-app screen first, including on a single image or video when a catalogue is connected. A stale Shopify price or a sold-out size on that screen is a feed problem. Purchases vanishing in Ads Manager, with sessions still arriving, is tracking, not this setting.

How do I turn Meta product browsing off after 28 October?

The notice opt-out closed on 28 October 2026. Edit each ad, turn off Add product browsing under Advantage+ creative, and turn it off under Destination, Personalised destinations, if it is listed. Publish, then check duplicated and new ads, because copies can come back with the default on. Change nothing else for a week, and judge Shopify orders rather than sessions.
