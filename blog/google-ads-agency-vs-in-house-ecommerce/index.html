Google Ads Agency vs In-House: What UK E-Commerce Stores Need to Know

Google Ads agency vs in-house | costs, expertise gaps and the honest answer for UK e-commerce store owners weighing both options.

Should I hire a Google Ads agency or manage it in-house for my e-commerce store?

For most UK e-commerce stores with monthly ad spend below £20,000, a specialist agency costs less than an experienced in-house hire and typically delivers better ROAS from month one. In-house management makes sense when you already have a full-time PPC specialist on payroll and consistent monthly spend above £30,000. If you are currently managing Google Ads yourself with no dedicated PPC experience, the opportunity cost of that time almost always outweighs the agency fee.

Whether to hire a Google Ads agency or keep it in-house is one of the most common questions we hear from UK e-commerce founders. It surfaces when spend is growing, when ROAS feels inconsistent, and when founders start wondering whether they are the right person to be sitting inside Ads Manager late at night. This guide covers the real cost comparison, the expertise gap that rarely gets mentioned in these discussions, when in-house management genuinely makes sense, and when it almost certainly does not.

What "Managing Google Ads In-House" Actually Involves

Most store owners who decide to handle Google Ads themselves start without a clear picture of what the work involves. A well-managed e-commerce account requires consistent attention: regular search term audits (to add negatives and cut irrelevant spend), bid strategy reviews, ad asset testing, product feed monitoring, conversion tracking validation, and keeping pace with platform updates. Performance Max has changed materially several times in recent years. Smart Bidding rules and asset requirements shift regularly. Staying current is a meaningful part of the job.

A competent PPC manager typically spends 8-15 hours per week on a mid-sized e-commerce account. If that is you as the founder, those are 8-15 hours pulled from product development, commercial strategy and operations every single week. Time has a cost that rarely appears on a P&L but compounds significantly over months.

The Real Cost of an In-House Google Ads Hire

If you are considering a dedicated in-house hire rather than managing it yourself, the salary benchmarks matter before you decide. An experienced Google Ads specialist in the UK earns £32,000-£55,000 per year in base salary. That figure excludes employer National Insurance contributions (currently 13.8%, as set by HMRC), pension contributions, paid leave, platform tool licences and ongoing training. The all-in cost of an experienced in-house PPC hire is typically £42,000-£70,000 per year.

Our Google Ads management fee is £1,500 per month, which is £18,000 per year. The fee difference alone is significant. A specialist agency also brings cross-account benchmarks from managing multiple e-commerce accounts simultaneously, early visibility on platform changes, and continuity: no holiday cover gaps, sick leave, or the onboarding lag that follows when an in-house hire leaves.

What About a Google Ads Freelancer?

A freelance Google Ads specialist sits between agency and in-house in cost, typically charging £500-£2,000 per month depending on experience and account complexity. Good freelancers are genuinely excellent and worth finding if you need a lighter-touch arrangement. The practical caveat is capacity: most experienced freelancers carry 8-15 clients simultaneously, which limits how much attention any single account receives in a given week.

The due-diligence checklist is identical regardless of route: confirm they will work inside accounts you own with full admin access, ask for case studies with specific revenue numbers rather than percentage claims, and check that their focus is e-commerce PPC rather than a broad mix of services. For more on what to watch for, the principles in our marketing agency red flags guide apply equally to freelancers.

When In-House Google Ads Management Genuinely Makes Sense

This is the part most agencies leave out. In-house management is the right call when:

  • You already have a full-time PPC specialist on payroll with verifiable Google Ads experience. Not a generalist digital marketer who "also handles PPC" alongside a dozen other responsibilities, but someone whose primary role is paid search.
  • Your monthly ad spend is consistently above £30,000-£50,000. At that level, a dedicated in-house hire can be justified on the numbers, particularly when your account needs daily attention across multiple markets or product ranges.
  • Your business has genuine internal complexity, such as custom ERP integrations or non-standard inventory systems, where deep contextual knowledge of your operations outweighs the pattern recognition an agency brings from managing other accounts.

Even in these situations, many larger stores retain a specialist agency for quarterly strategy reviews and independent audits to verify that the in-house team is not developing blind spots from working inside a single account.

When You Should Almost Certainly Use a Specialist Agency

In the accounts we manage, stores that arrive after a period of self-management tend to share a recognisable pattern. Performance Max campaigns spending against their broadest and least relevant search queries. Smart Bidding strategies set to target ROAS before the account has accumulated the 30-50 monthly conversions that Google's Smart Bidding documentation identifies as the minimum for reliable algorithm performance. Conversion tracking recording add-to-cart events or page views rather than completed purchases, which inflates reported ROAS and makes the account appear healthy while actual revenue growth stalls.

These are not unusual mistakes. They are the predictable outcomes when a powerful platform is managed without deep specialist experience. The cost is not just wasted spend in the moment: it is delayed growth across the entire period of poor account structure, sometimes running into months.

Does the Agency Fee Pay for Itself?

The right question is not "can I afford an agency?" It is: does specialist management improve results enough to cover its own cost? If you are spending £5,000 per month on Google Ads and structured management moves your ROAS from 3x to 5x, you have generated an additional £10,000 in attributed revenue from the same budget. A monthly fee of £1,500 has paid for itself several times over.

The benchmarks we publish reflect what specialist management of well-structured accounts can achieve: 15.2x average ROAS across the accounts we manage, with niche floors of 13x+ in cycling and 14x+ in outdoor. These figures are not a guarantee for every account. Starting performance depends on feed health, product margin and the conversion history already in the account. What they illustrate is the range of outcomes available when campaign structure and management are right. You can see more on our results page.

Four Questions to Answer Before You Decide

Whether you are comparing agency, freelance or in-house, these questions cut through the noise:

  • Does your reported ROAS match actual revenue in your store dashboard? A meaningful gap almost always means conversion tracking is broken. Nothing else is reliable until that is fixed first.
  • Who made the last meaningful structural change to your campaigns, and when? An account that has not been structurally reviewed in six months is almost certainly wasting a portion of its budget on preventable inefficiencies.
  • Do you own your Google Ads account with full admin access? If the answer is no, that is the first problem to resolve, regardless of everything else.
  • What does one hour of your time cost as a founder? Multiply that by the weekly hours currently going into ad management. Compare the result with an agency fee. The answer usually makes the decision straightforward.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is a Google Ads agency worth the cost for a small e-commerce store?

For most stores spending £2,000-£15,000 per month on Google Ads, a specialist agency pays for itself through improved ROAS and recovered wasted spend. The fee becomes harder to justify only if your spend is below £1,000-£1,500 per month (where it represents a large share of total spend) or if you already have a full-time in-house PPC specialist on payroll.

How much does a Google Ads manager cost in the UK?

An experienced in-house Google Ads manager costs £32,000-£55,000 in base salary, or £42,000-£70,000 all-in with employer National Insurance, pension and tools. A specialist agency typically charges £800-£3,000 per month. A freelance Google Ads specialist typically charges £500-£2,000 per month.

What is the main advantage of a Google Ads agency over in-house management?

Cross-account pattern recognition. An agency managing multiple e-commerce accounts in the same category can benchmark your performance against comparable stores, spot structural issues faster and apply learnings that an in-house hire managing a single account never accumulates.

Should I use a freelancer or an agency for Google Ads?

Both can deliver strong results. The key checks are identical: account ownership (you must own the ad account with full admin access), verifiable case studies with specific numbers, and a focus on e-commerce PPC rather than a broad mix of services. Agencies typically offer more continuity and capacity; freelancers often charge less.
