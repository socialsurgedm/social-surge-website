Performance Max Stopped Showing Brand Searches

Performance Max stopped showing brand searches, and the campaign ROAS often falls with them. Here is how to tell a brand-query shift from a real loss, and how to fund the Search campaign that should catch those queries.

If Performance Max stopped showing brand searches, the campaign can look broken while the shop is not. Brand clicks are cheap and convert easily, so when they leave Performance Max the ROAS on that campaign drops even if non-brand sales hold. In early November 2026 Google added a sentence to its help page saying Performance Max may filter navigational or brand-specific queries so a dedicated Search campaign can serve them. That sentence is no longer on the live page. What is still published is simpler: an exact-match Search keyword is preferred over Performance Max.

By Jack Goldsmith, Founder & Performance Marketer, Social Surge · 17 November 2026

In the accounts we manage, owners raise target ROAS in the fortnight before Black Friday because the tile dropped. That starves non-brand traffic that was never the problem.

Why did Performance Max stop showing brand searches?

Google's help page, How Performance Max interacts with other campaigns, still says a Search keyword that exactly matches the query, or is a spell-correction of it, is preferred over Performance Max. If nothing matches exactly, the higher Ad Rank serves.

What was new, briefly, was an extra paragraph. Search Engine Roundtable recorded it in early November 2026, and it was not in a June 2026 archive of the same page. The added text said Performance Max "may also filter out navigational or brand-specific search queries" so dedicated Search campaigns can serve them, provided those campaigns have sufficient budget and eligible ads. "May", not "will". No start date, no list of which queries count as brand, and no column that flags the filter.

As of 17 November 2026 that paragraph is gone. Treat it as wording Google published and then removed, not as a switch in the account. The pattern can still be real: brand queries inside Performance Max down, brand Search up, or brand queries gone if nothing eligible is left.

This is separate from brand exclusions, which you set. Google's instructions say that on Performance Max they cover Search, Shopping and YouTube search, with a checkbox to keep Shopping ads on excluded brands. If you turned exclusions on and funded no Search campaign, the drop is your setting. The other side of this, ROAS inflated by brand, is in Performance Max spending on your brand name.

Why did PMax ROAS drop in November 2026?

An illustration, not a client. Spend £10,000: £2,000 brand at 20x (£40,000) and £8,000 non-brand at 4x (£32,000). Blended ROAS is 7.2x. Remove brand and it reports 4x. The 7.2x was never the non-brand return.

If brand Search now carries that £40,000, revenue is flat and the campaign is cleaner. If brand Search is empty and orders are down by about that amount, the queries had nowhere to go. Cutting the Performance Max budget fixes neither.

Do not raise target ROAS the same week to chase 7.2x. Bidding spends less to hit a number it can no longer see. Why target ROAS misses applies harder after a mix change. If Ads purchases fell and the shop did not, fix tracking before bids. A new-customer value can push the same chart the other way, by adding value that is not cash.

Our published average is 18.3x ROAS at account level, not on the Performance Max campaign that just lost brand clicks. Judge orders and gross profit. The account results are on the case studies page.

Why is brand Search not picking up the queries?

The exact-match preference only helps a campaign that can actually serve. In the accounts we manage, brand Search misses the old Performance Max queries for four boring reasons.

  • No brand campaign. Performance Max was the only place the name could match. If it stops, coverage ends.
  • A shared budget. Non-brand spends it first. Brand is then limited by budget on queries that used to look free.
  • Phrase or broad, not exact. Only an exact match, or a spell-correction of one, is preferred over Performance Max. Phrase and broad compete on Ad Rank, and Performance Max can still win.
  • Ads that cannot run. A disapproved ad, a paused ad group, or a broken final URL means there is no impression to take.

The page matters too. Winning "your brand + rear light" and sending it home wastes the click. For a cycling or outdoor store in November, many brand queries are gifts, not people who already decided. Send them to the product.

Use a Search campaign of its own: exact match on the brand, the obvious misspelling, and brand plus the two or three product types selling this month. Give it a budget that is not shared, so lost impression share (budget) on those terms stays low. A sensible target rarely blocks brand. Eligibility and budget do.

Search themes will not put brand ahead of an exact-match keyword. They are optional, additive signals on an asset group (up to 50), with phrase and broad priority, and exclusions still apply. Use one only if you have no brand campaign tonight, then build Search and remove the theme.

What to check before you change the target

Compare 1-31 October with 1-16 November. In Performance Max, use Insights: search categories, plus the search terms sample. You will not see every query. You only need the direction of the brand category.

On brand Search, check impression share. Lost to budget means you found the leak. Lost to rank, with approved ads, points at the bid or the page.

Then look for a brand exclusion. If Shopping is blocked too, product ads on your name may have stopped, which is a bigger hole than text ads moving. Finish on shop orders. Flat orders mean a mix shift. Fewer orders, with brand Search flat, mean lost demand. If non-brand is down as well, check the feed and the prices instead. Change one thing, and wait two to three weeks before you touch target ROAS.

When you should not push brand back into Performance Max

If brand Search already has the volume and account revenue is flat, leave it. The lower ROAS is Performance Max without its easy clicks. Putting the brand back hides the non-brand number in the week you scale for Black Friday.

If almost nobody searches the name, a filter cannot be the cause. If you already excluded brand on purpose and exact-match Search is funded, do not undo it because November looks softer.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why did Performance Max stop showing brand searches?

In early November 2026 Google's help page briefly said Performance Max may filter out navigational or brand-specific search queries so a dedicated Search campaign can serve them. That line is not on the live page as of 17 November 2026. The rule still published: an exact-match Search keyword, or a spell-correction of one, is preferred over Performance Max. If brand Search is paused, disapproved or out of budget, those queries can stop.

Why did my PMax ROAS drop in November 2026?

Brand clicks are usually cheaper and convert more easily than non-brand clicks. If they leave Performance Max, campaign ROAS falls even when the products are unchanged. Compare account orders and the brand Search campaign before you cut budget or raise target ROAS. A lower ROAS with flat orders is a mix shift. A lower ROAS with missing orders is lost demand, or a tracking break.

Why is my brand Search campaign not picking up queries Performance Max used to take?

Usually there is no brand campaign, the budget is shared with non-brand, the ads are not eligible, or the keywords are phrase or broad rather than exact match. Only exact match, or a spell-correction of it, is preferred over Performance Max. Give brand Search its own budget and approved ads.

Should I add my brand as a Performance Max search theme?

Only if you have no brand Search campaign and need coverage tonight. Search themes are optional hints. They follow phrase and broad priority, not exact match, and exclusions still apply. They will not beat a funded exact-match brand campaign.
