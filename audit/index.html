What is a free PPC audit?

A free PPC audit is a no-obligation review of your ad account by Social Surge. With read-only access, we assess conversion tracking accuracy, wasted spend, campaign structure, shopping feed health and quick wins, then deliver written findings in plain English within a few working days. It's free, and you keep the findings.

What we check

Five Areas. Zero Fluff.

Tracking Accuracy

Is every conversion, and its true revenue value, actually being recorded? Most accounts we audit aren't. Bad data means bad bidding.

Wasted Spend

Queries and products consuming budget without ever converting. We quantify the waste in pounds per month, not vague percentages.

Campaign Structure

Overlapping campaigns, mixed bid strategies, everything-in-one-PMax, structural problems that cap your account's ceiling.

Feed Health

Titles, attributes and labels in your shopping feed, the raw material Google uses to decide when your products show.

Quick Wins

Changes you (or we) could make this week for immediate impact, clearly separated from the longer structural work.

Five areas: conversion tracking accuracy, wasted spend, campaign structure, shopping feed health and quick wins, quantified in pounds where possible, delivered as written findings in plain English.
