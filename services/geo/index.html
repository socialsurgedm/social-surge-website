What is GEO (generative engine optimisation)?

Generative engine optimisation (GEO) is the practice of improving how often and how accurately AI assistants such as ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Copilot mention and recommend a brand. It combines clear, citable content on your own site, consistent brand information across the web, and mentions in the third-party sources those assistants rely on.

What’s included

  • Competitor visibility comparison
  • Consistent entity information across the web
  • Citable content: definitions, data and comparisons
  • Structured data and crawler access checks
  • Third-party mention and listing plan

Questions
How is GEO different from SEO?

SEO targets rankings in search results. GEO targets the answers AI assistants write, which draw on many sources at once, so mentions on other sites and consistent brand information matter as much as your own pages.

Can you guarantee ChatGPT will recommend us?

No one can guarantee that. We measure how often you’re mentioned for the questions that matter and work to increase it.

Do AI crawlers need access to our site?

Usually, yes. We check your robots.txt and hosting so the crawlers used by AI search tools can read your pages.
