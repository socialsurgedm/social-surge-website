FAQ

Google Ads Management Questions

How much does Google Ads management cost UK?

UK Google Ads management typically costs £800-£3,000+ per month, and many agencies add a percentage of ad spend on top. We charge a flat £1,500/month per ad account, no spend percentage, no long-term contract, no hidden fees. Full breakdown on our pricing page.

What is a good ROAS for Google Shopping?

For most e-commerce retailers, 3-4x is solid and 5x+ is strong, the right target depends on your margins. Our client accounts average 18.3x ROAS, and our best single month was 35.3x for a UK cycling retailer. See the case studies for verified numbers.

Do you work with Performance Max?

Yes, PMax is central to most e-commerce accounts we run. The difference is how we structure it: clean feeds, product-level exclusions and realistic tROAS targets so the algorithm scales winners rather than burning budget on products that never convert.

Will you fix our conversion tracking?

Yes, and it's step one. We audit and rebuild GA4 and Google Tag Manager tracking before optimising anything. If revenue data is wrong, Smart Bidding optimises towards the wrong outcomes, fixing tracking is often the single highest-impact change we make.

How quickly will we see results?

Structural fixes, tracking corrections, wasted-spend removal, campaign consolidation, usually show impact within two weeks. Meaningful ROAS improvement typically lands within 60-90 days as feed optimisation and tROAS bidding mature.

Do you take a percentage of ad spend?

No. Flat monthly fees only. Percentage-of-spend pricing rewards agencies for spending more, not performing better, we think that's a conflict of interest, so we don't do it.
