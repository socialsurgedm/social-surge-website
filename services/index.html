The Social Surge model

AI Infrastructure + Human Management. Together.

Most agencies pick a lane: humans clicking buttons, or software nobody checks. We run both, and each makes the other better.

What the AI layer does

  • Analyses every account continuously, 30/60/90-day performance cycles, not monthly snapshots
  • Flags anomalies the moment spend, CPA or conversion rate drifts outside normal ranges
  • Surfaces budget scaling opportunities backed by product-level ROAS data
  • Generates automated weekly reports and change-history intelligence

What the humans do

  • Judge which signals matter, margin context, stock levels, seasonality the data alone can't see
  • Build and restructure campaigns, feeds, audiences and creative tests
  • Talk to you, monthly strategy calls, plain-English reporting, honest recommendations
  • Own the outcome. Software doesn't take responsibility for your ROAS. We do.
