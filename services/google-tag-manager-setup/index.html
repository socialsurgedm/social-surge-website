Questions
Why does tracking matter so much for ads?

Google and Meta bid using the conversions you send them. Missing or wrong values mean the algorithms optimise for the wrong things.

Do I need Consent Mode v2?

If you advertise to users in the UK or EEA with Google, Consent Mode v2 is needed to keep measurement and remarketing working within consent rules.

Will you break our existing tracking?

No. Changes are built and tested in preview mode and checked by a specialist before anything is published.
