FAQ

AI Infrastructure Questions

What does "AI infrastructure" actually mean?

It's a fully managed intelligence layer running continuously on your ad account data, dedicated compute, LLM hosting, automated analysis pipelines and alerting, all built and operated by us. It is not a SaaS dashboard, a browser plugin or a script you have to run yourself. You receive the outputs; we run the machinery.

How is this different from a reporting dashboard?

Dashboards show numbers and leave the interpretation to you. Our infrastructure analyses the numbers: it detects anomalies, explains what changed and why, correlates change history with performance shifts, and produces written recommendations, automatically, on a schedule.

Is my ad account data secure?

Yes. Your data runs on dedicated latest-generation Mac hardware we own and operate, not shared VPS instances. Access is read-only via official ad platform APIs, data is segregated per client, and Scale-tier clients can opt for local deployment on their own premises. Enterprise deployments run entirely on your infrastructure.

How is this different from just hiring an agency?

An agency gives you people; infrastructure gives those people superhuman coverage. The AI layer analyses every account every day, something no human team does economically. Many clients run it alongside our PPC management; others use it standalone to sharpen their in-house team or existing agency.

How much does it cost?

All prices include VAT: Starter £625/month + £1,250 setup (1-5 accounts), Growth £2,250/month + £4,500 setup (up to 10 accounts), Scale £6,000/month + £12,000 setup (15+ accounts). Enterprise private deployments run £30,000-£75,000 one-time plus £1,000-£2,500/month maintenance. Annual commitments earn 10-20% discounts, locked API pricing and priority servicing.
