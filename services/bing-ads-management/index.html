Questions
What is the difference between Bing Ads and Google Ads?

Microsoft Advertising runs on Bing, Yahoo and MSN, which together account for around 6 to 10% of UK search volume. CPCs are typically lower than Google, competition is lower in many niches, and Microsoft uniquely allows targeting by LinkedIn profile data including job title, industry and company size. It works best alongside Google rather than instead of it.

Does Microsoft Advertising work for e-commerce?

Yes, particularly for higher-ticket products where the Microsoft audience skews older and more professional. Microsoft Shopping campaigns work in the same way as Google Shopping and can feed directly from your existing product feed.

Who owns the Microsoft Advertising account?

You do. We work inside your account with full transparency, so every change and every number is visible to you.
