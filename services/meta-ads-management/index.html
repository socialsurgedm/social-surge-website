FAQ

Meta Ads Management Questions

How much does a Facebook Ads agency cost in the UK?

UK Facebook/Meta Ads management typically costs £600-£2,500+ per month. We charge a flat £1,000/month per Meta account, or £2,250/month for combined Google + Meta management, no percentage of ad spend. Full detail on our pricing page.

Is Facebook advertising still worth it for e-commerce?

Yes, when tracking is done properly. With Conversion API in place and a structured creative testing programme, Meta remains one of the most scalable acquisition channels for e-commerce, especially for demand generation alongside Google's demand capture.

How do you handle iOS 14.5+ tracking limitations?

We implement Meta's Conversion API (server-side tracking) alongside the pixel, verify your domain and configure prioritised events. This recovers a significant share of the conversion signal lost to Apple's privacy changes, so the algorithm optimises on real purchase data.

Do you create the ad creative?

We run the creative testing programme, angles, hooks, formats and iteration schedules, and provide clear briefs on what to produce next based on test data. We work with your existing product imagery and content, and tell you exactly what creative is worth investing in.

Should I run Google Ads and Meta Ads together?

For most e-commerce brands, yes. Meta puts your products in front of new audiences; Google captures people already searching to buy. Our combined package manages both for £2,250/month with unified strategy and reporting.
