What is Social Surge?

Social Surge is a Bristol-based growth agency for e-commerce brands, online retailers and DTC brands in any niche, doing £1M+ a year or close to it and advertising on Google, Meta or both. It also helps service-based businesses. Its team manages Google Ads and Meta Ads hands-on, while email marketing, SEO, AEO, GEO and Google Tag Manager setup are run by its AI infrastructure with a specialist reviewing every change. Clients get flat fees, full account access, weekly reporting and rolling monthly agreements, with an 18.3x average ROAS across managed accounts.

Questions
How long before I see results?

Most accounts improve measurably within 30 days, with meaningful ROAS growth by 60 to 90 days. Tracking and wasted-spend fixes often show in the first fortnight.

How much does it cost?

Google Ads and Meta Ads management each start from £1,650 a month, or from £2,250 for both. Your exact fee depends on your business, such as revenue, ad spend and scope, and it’s always a flat fee, never a percentage of spend. Full pricing.

Do you require a contract?

No. Every agreement rolls monthly. Clients stay because the results justify it.

Who owns the ad accounts?

You do, always. You get full access and can check every number we report.

What makes you different?

We work with online retailers and DTC brands, and help service-based businesses win more enquiries. Paid media is run hands-on, everything else is run by our AI infrastructure with a specialist checking every change, and every client gets full transparency.
