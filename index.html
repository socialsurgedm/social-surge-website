FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How long before I see results from PPC?

Most e-commerce accounts see measurable improvement within 30 days and meaningful ROAS growth within 60-90 days. Structural fixes, tracking corrections, campaign consolidation, stopping wasted spend, often show impact in the first two weeks.

How much does a PPC agency cost in the UK?

UK PPC agency fees typically range from £800 to £3,000+ per month depending on ad spend and scope. Our pricing is published openly: Google Ads management from £1,500/month, Meta Ads from £1,000/month, combined from £2,250/month. See our pricing page for full detail.

What's your approach to Google Ads management?

Foundation first: accurate tracking, clean campaign structure and feed optimisation. Then data-led scaling, product-level exclusions, tROAS bidding and budget allocation driven by margin, not vanity metrics.

Do you require long-term contracts?

No. Rolling monthly agreements only. Clients stay because performance justifies it, not because a contract forces it.

How do you measure success?

ROAS, CPA, conversion value and revenue growth, reported weekly with full account access so you can verify every number yourself.

What makes you different from other PPC agencies?

PPC-only specialisation, 100% ad account transparency, niche e-commerce expertise, and an AI performance infrastructure layer that continuously analyses your accounts, something no template agency offers.
