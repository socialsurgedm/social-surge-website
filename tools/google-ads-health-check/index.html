How do I know if my Google Ads account is healthy?

A healthy Google Ads account sends accurate revenue values to Google, reviews search terms weekly, manages negative keywords, uses an optimised product feed, bids to targets based on margin, excludes non-selling products, is optimised regularly and is reported on weekly.
