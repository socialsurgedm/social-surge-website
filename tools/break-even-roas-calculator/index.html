How do you calculate break-even ROAS?

Break-even ROAS is 1 divided by your gross margin after fees and delivery. At a 40% margin, break-even ROAS is 2.5x. To keep a set profit after ad spend, divide 1 by your margin minus the profit you want: at a 40% margin and 10% profit goal, the target is 1 ÷ 0.30 = 3.33x.

The formula

Break-even ROAS is 1 divided by your gross margin. At a 40% margin you need £2.50 of revenue for every £1 of ad spend just to break even. To keep 10% profit after ads, divide 1 by the margin minus the profit you want: 1 ÷ (0.40 − 0.10) = 3.33x.
