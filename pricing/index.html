FAQ

Pricing Questions

How much should I pay a PPC agency?

UK PPC agencies typically charge £800-£3,000+ per month, often plus 10-20% of ad spend. As a benchmark: we charge £1,500/month flat for Google Ads, £1,000/month for Meta, £2,250/month combined. If an agency won't publish its pricing, ask yourself why.

Do you take a percentage of ad spend?

No. Flat monthly fees only. Percentage-of-spend pricing rewards agencies for spending more of your money, not for performing better, we consider that a conflict of interest and refuse to price that way.

Is PPC management worth it?

If the agency lifts performance by more than its fee, yes. At £1,500/month on a £10,000/month Google Ads account, a modest efficiency gain covers the fee. Our client average is 18.3x ROAS, and structural fixes alone often recover more than the fee in wasted spend. See verified case studies.

Are there any long-term contracts?

No. All PPC management runs on rolling monthly agreements. AI infrastructure clients can optionally commit annually in exchange for a 10-20% discount, locked API pricing and priority servicing, but it's a choice, not a requirement.

What does the ad-hoc consulting rate cover?

£150/hour, billed in 30-minute increments. One-off audits, tracking fixes, campaign builds, training and second opinions, useful if you run ads in-house and need targeted expert input without a retainer.

Are there any hidden costs?

No. Your ad spend goes directly to Google or Meta from your own billing, we never touch it. The fees on this page are the complete cost of working with us. AI infrastructure prices include VAT; PPC management fees are subject to VAT where applicable.
