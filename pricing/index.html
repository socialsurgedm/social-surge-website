How much does a PPC agency cost in the UK?

UK PPC agencies typically charge £800 to £3,000+ a month, often plus 10 to 20% of ad spend. Social Surge charges flat fees only, never a percentage of spend. Google Ads management starts from £1,650 a month, Meta Ads from £1,650, both combined from £2,250, and AI performance infrastructure from £1,105 including VAT. The exact fee depends on the business, including revenue, ad spend, number of accounts and scope, and is confirmed in writing after a free audit.

Pricing questions
Why are prices shown as “from”?

Every business is different. Your fee is based on your revenue, ad spend, number of ad accounts, catalogue size and the scope you need. We confirm a fixed monthly figure in writing after your free audit, before you commit.

Do you take a percentage of ad spend?

No. Flat monthly fees only. Percentage-of-spend pricing rewards agencies for spending more of your money, not for performing better.

Are there long-term contracts?

No. PPC management runs on rolling monthly agreements. AI infrastructure clients can choose an annual commitment for a 10 to 20% discount.

Is PPC management worth it?

If the agency lifts performance by more than its fee, yes. On a £10,000 a month Google Ads account, a modest efficiency gain covers a fee starting at £1,650, and fixing wasted spend alone often recovers more than that.

What does ad-hoc consulting cover?

£150 an hour, billed in 30-minute blocks: one-off audits, tracking fixes, campaign builds, training and second opinions, with no retainer.

Are there hidden costs?

No. Your ad spend goes straight to Google or Meta from your own billing. PPC management fees are subject to VAT where applicable; AI infrastructure prices include VAT.
