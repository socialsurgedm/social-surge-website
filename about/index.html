About us

The Agency That Only Does One Thing

Social Surge DM LTD is a PPC-only e-commerce agency based in Bristol. No web design upsells, no social media packages, no SEO retainers. Paid media for online retailers, done properly, measured honestly.

Who is Social Surge?

Social Surge is a Bristol-based, PPC-only e-commerce agency founded by performance marketer Jack Goldsmith. It manages Google Ads, Meta Ads and AI performance infrastructure for UK online retailers, operating on flat fees, rolling monthly agreements and full client account access, with an 18.3x average ROAS across its client base.

Why Social Surge Exists

Social Surge was founded by Jack Goldsmith, a performance marketer who kept seeing the same story in e-commerce ad accounts: broken conversion tracking feeding bad data to Smart Bidding, budgets spread across products that never sell, and monthly PDF reports designed to obscure rather than explain.

The fix wasn't complicated, it was disciplined. Accurate tracking first. Wasted spend cut second. Then structured scaling, product by product, driven by margin data rather than vanity metrics. Applied consistently, that methodology turned a Search-only account into a £4.3M record-breaking year and produced client months as high as 35.3x ROAS.

So Social Surge does one thing: PPC for e-commerce. That specialisation, plus a proprietary AI performance infrastructure that analyses client accounts continuously, is the entire business model. When paid media is all you do, you get very good at it.

The methodology

Foundation Waste Structure Scale

Every account we take on follows the same named sequence, because it works, verifiably.

Foundation

GA4/GTM tracking rebuilt so every conversion and its true revenue value is measured before anything is optimised.

Waste

Zero-conversion products excluded, junk queries negated. One cycling client: 304 exclusions, +34% ROAS.

Structure

Campaigns rebuilt by margin and performance tier; feeds optimised for Shopping and Performance Max.

Scale

Budgets grow only where tROAS holds. One client grew £2.5M in revenue in 12 months on this sequence.
