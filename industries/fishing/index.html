Why Fishing PPC Is Different

Angling e-commerce punishes lazy account management. Here's what we do differently.

Vast SKU Catalogues

Tackle retailers can carry tens of thousands of SKUs across coarse, carp, sea and game disciplines. We structure feeds and campaigns by discipline, brand and margin with custom labels, so every product line gets a bid strategy that matches how it actually sells.

Match-the-Hatch Intent

Anglers search like specialists: exact rod models, reel sizes, line breaking strains, bait flavours. Generic keyword targeting wastes money here. We build campaigns around the niche, specific queries that convert, and let the vague ones go.

Negative Keyword Strategy

Real result: for one UK fishing retailer we added 996 exact-match negatives in a single structured pass, a projected £1,618/month saving in wasted spend, while 127 high-ROAS keywords were explicitly protected from being blocked.

Precision Over Volume

Broad negatives kill winners; no negatives fund junk traffic. Our exact-match approach removes waste surgically while the keywords driving revenue stay untouched, the discipline behind our 28x+ ROAS months in fishing.
