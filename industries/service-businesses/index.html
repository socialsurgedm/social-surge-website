How does Social Surge help service businesses?

Social Surge helps service-based businesses get more qualified enquiries from Google Ads, Meta Ads and search. It sets up tracking for calls, forms and bookings, targets the searches people make when they’re ready to buy, filters out low-quality leads, and reports cost per enquiry rather than clicks.

What we do for service businesses

  • Call, form and booking tracking in GA4 and Google Ads
  • Search campaigns on high-intent and local searches
  • Negative keywords to filter jobseekers, DIY and price-only searches
  • Meta lead campaigns with quality filters
  • Email nurture for enquiries that don’t book straight away
  • Local SEO, AEO and GEO so you’re found and recommended
  • Reporting on cost per enquiry and cost per booked job

Questions
Do you only work with e-commerce?

No. Our core is online retail and DTC brands, and we also help service-based businesses that want more qualified enquiries.

Can you track phone calls?

Yes. We track calls from ads and from your website, alongside forms and online bookings, so you can see which campaigns produce real enquiries.

How do you stop poor-quality leads?

With negative keywords, tighter targeting, qualifying questions on forms and by optimising campaigns on enquiries that turn into bookings rather than all form fills.
